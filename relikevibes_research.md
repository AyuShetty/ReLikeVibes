| --- | --- | --- |

| A public LinkedIn post frames the channel as a community of **over 1.6M subscribers**. | Public profile activity: [LinkedIn](https://in.linkedin.com/in/abhay-sreejith-b744b0246) | Use community as the scale narrative, rather than treating the number as a disconnected vanity metric. |

## Recognition Direction

The revised experience will use **brushed silver** as the material language of the 100K Creator Award and **warm gold** as the material language of the 1M Creator Award. The interaction model will replace the generic signal-lime instrumentation with a record-sleeve / award-plaque system: silver resolves the curatorial archive, while gold marks scale, cultural reach, and milestone moments. These will be visual metaphors only; the website will not reproduce YouTube trademarks or award designs.

## Research Limitation

Instagram required a login during direct inspection, so its profile bio is treated as search-discovered context rather than a primary verified source. The redesign will rely on official YouTube facts for public channel scale and format, and use the founder profile only to inform tone.
